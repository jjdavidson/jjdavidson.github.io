# The De Grey-Harder 5-Chromatic UDG

{% include demos/embed.html name="harder-construction" id="moncay-harders-construction" %}

This visualization follows Jannis Harder’s two-copy simplification of Aubrey de Grey’s 2018 construction of a five-chromatic unit-distance graph. During Polymath16, Harder found that a graph built from de Grey’s unit directions forces the origin and a point $8/3$ units away to share a color in every proper four-coloring. The visualization builds this graph from rotated hexagons, then turns a second copy about the shared origin until the two outer marked points are one unit apart. Joining those points by a unit edge makes a four-coloring impossible. The final step shows a proper five-coloring. [Harder’s April 28, 2018 Polymath16 comment](https://dustingmixon.wordpress.com/2018/04/22/polymath16-second-thread-what-does-it-take-to-be-5-chromatic/#comment-4074)
