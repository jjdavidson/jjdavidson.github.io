## Research Experience

**Current Research in Combinatorics**

- Investigating monomial Cayley graphs as an algebraic approach to unit-distance graph coloring and the Hadwiger–Nelson problem.
- Studying extremal edge densities in Hamming graphs as alphabet size and dimension vary, connecting classical and hypercube extremal theory.
- Collaborating on monochromatic rectangle-free grid colorings and odd representations of hypergraphs.
- Using computational experimentation, SAT-based searches, and flag algebra methods to investigate constructions, conjectures, and extremal bounds.

**Master’s Research in Combinatorics**<br>
California State University, Los Angeles \| January 2024 – December 2025

- Investigated extremal problems on Hamming and other product graphs under the direction of Jason O’Neill.
- Studied matrix methods for Abelian Cayley graphs with Mike Krebs.
- Explored the Union-Closed Conjecture, the Erdős Box Problem, Oddtown-type problems, and hypercube extremal questions.

**Stanford University Research in Mathematics (SURIM)**<br>
Stanford University \| Summer 2016

- Researched equilibrium capillary surfaces with Robert Finn.
