# Set Representations

Set representations encode graphs and hypergraphs through intersection patterns of families of sets. My work studies odd representations of hypergraphs, where parity conditions make Oddtown-type arguments and linear algebra especially useful.

## Current Work

- **Odd Representations of Hypergraphs** — Manuscript in preparation, joint with Griffin Johnston, Jason O’Neill, and G. Ruiz-Navarro.

## Background Articles

- **[Oddtown and Modtown]({{ '/research/oddtown.html' | relative_url }})** — Intersection conditions, the linear algebra method, and modular variants that provide background for odd representations.

## Planned Background Articles

- **Set Representations of Graphs** — Intersection representations and the size of the ground set, beginning with the [Erdős–Goodman–Pósa paper](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1966-21.pdf).
- **Set Representations of Hypergraphs** — Extending the graph perspective to hypergraphs, including the bounded-degree setting studied by [Basu, Johnston, Rödl, and Sales](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/view/v32i1p27).
- **Graham–Pollak** — Biclique partitions, algebraic lower bounds, and how the classical theorem motivates odd covering problems.
- **Odd Coverings** — Parity-based covers of graphs and hypergraphs and their relationship to odd set representations, with [Odd Covers of Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09822) as background.
- **Tensor Rank** — Rank-one tensor decompositions and the connection between tensor rank and the odd-covering problems studied in our work.
- **Circuit Complexity and the Fusion Method** — Cover formulations of computational models and their role in circuit lower bounds, introducing [Wigderson’s fusion-method viewpoint](https://simons.berkeley.edu/talks/circuit-lower-bounds-more-fusion-method).
- **Odd Representations** — How parity changes the representation problem, why Oddtown arguments enter, and the questions motivating our joint work.
