# Cayley Graphs

Cayley graphs describe adjacency through a group and a choice of generators. My work uses this algebraic structure to study graph coloring, from circulants to monomial Cayley graphs arising in the Hadwiger–Nelson problem.

## Papers

- **[Coloring Monomial Cayley Graphs: The One-Parameter Case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09474)**
- **[Chromatic numbers of circulants with indispensable generators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23841)**

## Planned Background Articles

- **Cayley and Circulant Graphs** — Groups, generating sets, and the effect of generators on chromatic number, providing background for the circulants paper.
- **Unit-Distance Graph Coloring** — The Hadwiger–Nelson problem, finite witnesses, and the role of geometric configurations in proving coloring bounds.
- **Monomial Cayley Graphs** — The algebraic construction behind my paper and how finite configurations and computational searches help reveal its coloring properties.
