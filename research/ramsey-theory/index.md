# Ramsey and Extremal Problems on Grids

My work on monochromatic rectangle-free colorings of grids connects Ramsey theory, extremal combinatorics, and combinatorial designs. The background articles in this section introduce the problems and constructions behind that work.

## Current Work

- **Optimal Monochromatic Rectangle Free Colorings of Grids** — Manuscript in preparation, joint with Jason O’Neill and G. Ruiz-Navarro.

## Planned Background Articles

- **Monochromatic Rectangles** — Grid colorings, their interpretation as edge colorings of complete bipartite graphs, and the rectangle-avoidance question behind our paper.
- **The Zarankiewicz Problem** — Forbidden configurations in bipartite graphs and matrices, and how extremal bounds constrain individual color classes in a grid coloring.
- **The Erdős Box Problem** — Cartesian products in grids, box-free sets, and higher-dimensional extensions of rectangle-avoidance questions.
- **Gallai–Witt** — Ramsey theory for finite patterns in colored lattices and its connections to grid-coloring questions.
- **Design Constructions for Grid Colorings** — How combinatorial designs can organize color classes and provide constructions for avoiding monochromatic rectangles.

My **[Design Theory notes]({{ '/notes/design-theory/design-theory.html' | relative_url }})** provide related background on the combinatorial structures used in these constructions.
