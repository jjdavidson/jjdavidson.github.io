# Extremal Theory

How many edges can we keep in a graph while avoiding a prescribed configuration? My work focuses on this question in Hamming graphs, which provide a setting for connecting classical extremal graph theory with extremal problems in the hypercube.

## Current Work

- **Turán Densities in Hamming Graphs** — Manuscript in preparation, developing questions from my master's research.
- **[Extremal Problems on Hamming Graphs]({{ '/assets/pdf/Masters_Thesis.pdf' | relative_url }})** — My master's thesis.

## Planned Background Articles

- **Turán Theory** — Forbidden subgraphs, extremal numbers, and edge densities: the classical questions motivating the Hamming graph setting.
- **Extremal Problems in Hypercubes** — The structure of the hypercube and how its growing dimension shapes forbidden-subgraph problems.
- **Hamming Graphs** — Cartesian products of complete graphs, the roles of alphabet size and dimension, and the connection between classical and hypercube extremal theory.
- **Computational Methods for Extremal Densities** — Using finite examples, computational experiments, and flag algebra methods to investigate constructions and bounds relevant to this project.
