# Research

My research focuses on graph coloring, extremal problems, and set representations of graphs and hypergraphs. These pages bring together my papers, ongoing projects, and background articles that introduce the questions and methods behind them.

The articles are organized into four areas:

- **[Cayley Graphs]({{ '/research/cayley-graphs/' | relative_url }})** — Coloring monomial Cayley graphs and circulants, with connections to the Hadwiger–Nelson problem.
- **[Extremal Theory]({{ '/research/extremal-theory/' | relative_url }})** — Turán densities in Hamming graphs and extremal problems in hypercubes.
- **[Set Representations]({{ '/research/set-representations/' | relative_url }})** — Odd representations of hypergraphs, set intersections, and Oddtown-type arguments.
- **[Ramsey Theory]({{ '/research/ramsey-theory/' | relative_url }})** — Monochromatic rectangle-free grids and their connections to extremal problems and combinatorial designs.

Each area begins with the related papers or manuscripts, followed by background articles intended to help readers get up to speed. The planned articles focus on tools and problems that connect directly to these projects.

The first background article, **[Oddtown and Modtown]({{ '/research/oddtown.html' | relative_url }})**, introduces intersection conditions and the linear algebra method used in my work on odd representations. Full publication details are available on my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
